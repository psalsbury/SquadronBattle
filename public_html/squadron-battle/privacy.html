Squadron Battle Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Squadron Battle app for Android and iPhone (uk.salsbury.squadronbattle) and the Squadron Battle game at salsbury.uk/squadron-battle. Squadron Battle is made by Pete Salsbury, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR).

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Signing in (with Google, or with Apple on iPhone) is optional and only needed for the leaderboards and the daily sortie board.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • The app has no adverts and no purchases.
  • We don't sell your data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the stages you've unlocked, your best scores, your daily sortie bests and settings such as sound, music and controls. This data stays in the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Data our server receives

The app loads the daily sortie and the leaderboards from salsbury.uk. Like any website, the server keeps a standard access log of each request: your IP address, the time, the page requested and your device's browser identifier ("user agent"). The logs are kept for up to 14 days and used only to keep the service running and secure.

We also count visits and games played to understand how the games are used. Before anything is stored, the IP address and user agent are combined and scrambled (hashed with a secret value), so the counts can't be traced back to you. We keep only totals.

Signing in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account name, email address and profile picture link. If you use Sign in with Apple, we store the name you choose to share and the email address Apple gives us, which may be a private relay address. When you're signed in, finished games send your score, the stage reached, the difficulty, pilots rescued, whether you won and how long the run took, and daily sorties send their score. Your first name, picture and scores appear on the public leaderboards. We use the email address only to identify your account.

You can delete your account at any time with Delete my account at the bottom of the app's home screen (or at salsbury.uk/account), which removes your account and your scores in every salsbury.uk game.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. The only third parties involved are Google or Apple (only if you choose to sign in with them) and our hosting provider, which runs the server and processes data only on our behalf.

Children

Squadron Battle is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
